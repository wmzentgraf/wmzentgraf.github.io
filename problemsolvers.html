Geo’s Problem-Solver Cookies

Yield:

1. Start

Cream together on high-speed for 2 minutes:

200 gOrganic cane sugar
105 gLight brown sugar
105 gDark brown sugar
113 g (1 stick)Unsalted butter, melted and browned to 100g
113 g (1 stick)Unsalted butter, cold and chopped into small cubes
If vegan, substitute warm butter for coconut oil and cold butter for your preferred brand of plant-based butter, cubed.

2. Wet Ingredients

Add and mix 30 seconds:

2Eggs
1 tblVanilla
If vegan, substitute eggs for unsweetened applesauce (1/4 cup aka 2 oz aka 4 tbl == 1 egg in most dessert baking).

3. Dry Ingredients

Add (all whisked together first), and fold by-hand just until you don’t see any white:

360 g (3 c)All-purpose flour
120 g (1 c)Cake flour
1 tblCornstarch
2 teaBaking powder
2 teaBaking soda
1 teaFine salt

4. Chocolate Chips

Add 1.5-2 cups chocolate chips, about a bag (semi-sweet) and a half bag (dark), save the other half bag. There is such a thing as too many chocolate chips in the dough and each cookie gets more later.

5. Refridgerate & Pre-Heat

Put the dough in an airtight container and refridgerate ~20 minutes.

Set your oven to 350F/177C before you start cleaning up.

325F/108C if convection, which is preferred for nearly all baking.

6. Baking

Measure balls of dough, 33-36g, roll, place on parchment paper. Firmly press 3 chocolate chips into center of cookie.

Freeze the tray (with the cookies) for 5-10 minutes! Then sprinkle the dimple with coarse salt.

Bake, ~8-10 min, remove the moment the edges are light brown, easy to over bake, they’re done when you smell them in another room.

If you went beyond 11 min 11 sec you burnt them or your oven isn’t actually the number it says it is.

7. The Smack

When removing the cookies, firmly grip one end of the pan and smack/drop the cookies down on a hard safe surface (NOT A GLASS TOP), once. This should flatten the puffy middle making for the best chewy texture.

8. Cooling & Storage

Let cool on pan 5-10 min, then transfer to rack, store in sealed container.

Refrigerate leftover cookie dough in sealed container, shape cookies while dough is cold.
